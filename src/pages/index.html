01 · WHY IT STAYS HIDDEN

Younger adults are the least likely to know.

In the ABS National Health Survey, nurses measured people's blood pressure and asked whether they had been told it was high. Among 18 to 44 year olds who measured high, more than 9 in 10 didn't report it. Older people are measured more often, so more of them know.

HiddenPressure applies these rates to the number of adults of each age in every council (ABS Census 2021).

03 · WHERE TO CHECK

Put stations where people pass, not just where councils are big.

The obvious plan puts one station in each of the biggest councils. The planner instead picks, one at a time, the venue that reaches the most people not already covered. Busy shopping centres and stations near borders reach two or three councils at once.

    04 · IN THEIR LANGUAGE

    A poster for every venue, in the languages spoken nearby.

    Each poster uses the top languages spoken at home in the venue's council. Every translation is marked as a draft for a community translator to check, and languages without a checked line say so instead of guessing.
