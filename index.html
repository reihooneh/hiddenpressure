HiddenPressure

About three in four Australians with high blood pressure don't report having it. HiddenPressure estimates where those people live, council by council, using 2021 Census data, then plans where free blood pressure check stations should go to reach the most of them, with outreach posters in the five languages each community speaks most at home.

    How the estimate works

    For each council, adults in seven age groups (2021 Census) are multiplied by the share of that age group with high blood pressure when measured, then by the share of those who did not report having it (ABS National Health Survey 2017–18). Across these councils that gives 74% unreported, matching the national 73.7%.

    How the plan is chosen

    Each venue reaches nearby residents, fading with distance, and busier venue types reach more people. Overlapping stations share people instead of double counting. A greedy optimiser adds, one at a time, the station that reaches the most extra undiagnosed people, a standard method for this "maximum coverage" problem.

    Limits

    • Estimates assume national rates by age apply locally. Real rates differ with culture, income and access to care.
    • Council-level data is coarse. The full version uses suburb-level (SA2) Census tables.
    • Venue reach is a simplified model, not measured foot traffic.
    • Poster translations are drafts until checked by native speakers.
